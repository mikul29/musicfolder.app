Made by musicians, for musicians.

Music Folder began with a pile of folders—and a problem you might recognize.

I’ve played in bands, orchestras, and jazz groups, and every group seemed to come with another folder. Before long, I had loose pages everywhere, set lists to rearrange, and a knack for bringing every chart except the one I needed.

The musicians around me knew the feeling. We were all trying to keep our music organized while juggling separate apps for a metronome, tuner, and pitch pipe. I wanted something simple, practical, and affordable that brought it all together.

So I built Music Folder.

It started as a place for my own songs, books, and set lists, with the tools I reached for built right in. When I shared it with other musicians, it became a way to help them with the same frustrations that got me started.

Have a question, or an idea that would make Music Folder better? I’d love to hear from you.

Matthew MikulichMusician and developer of Music Folder
