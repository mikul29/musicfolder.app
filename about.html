Made by a musician, for musicians.

Music Folder is an independent app, designed, built and supported by one person.

Music Folder started with a simple idea: everything you play from should live in one place, ready the moment you need it, whether that's a practice room, a rehearsal or a stage.

It has grown into a complete home for your music: a library of songs, books and folders, set lists that play straight through, Apple Pencil markings, a full practice toolkit and live sharing with the people you play with.

Every feature, every design decision and every support email is handled by the same person. If you write in, you'll hear back from the developer.

Matthew MikulichDeveloper of Music Folder

What Music Folder stands for.

Your music stays yours

No data collected and no account to create. Your music and notes live on your device and in your own iCloud.

Ready without a signal

Everything works offline, from the reader to set lists to every tool.

Fair pricing

Free for up to 15 songs, with iCloud sync free for everyone. If you bought Music Folder before version 3.0, you keep full access.

Built with musicians

Features come from the people who use it. Tell me what you need.
