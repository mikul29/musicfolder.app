Features

Your whole library, in one place.

Import PDFs from Files, AirDrop or email, sort them into folders and tags, and scan paper charts.

Your set, in order.

Build a set for every gig or service, then play straight through it, turning pages with a Bluetooth pedal.

Mark it up like paper.

Write on the score with Apple Pencil. Every mark saves and syncs.

Works with Planning Center.

Connect your Planning Center Services account and your team's songs and upcoming services appear next to your set lists, ready to play.

Work together as a team.

Share a folder or set list with your band, class, choir or worship team. Changes show up for everyone, and you choose who can edit.
