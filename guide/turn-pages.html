Turn pages

On this page
  • Swipe to turn a page
  • Use a keyboard
  • Read ahead with Half-Page Turns

You can turn pages in the reader with a swipe, with a keyboard, or half a page at a time with Half-Page Turns, which lets you read the top of the next page while you finish the current one. To turn pages with a Bluetooth pedal or with your face, see Turn pages hands-free.

Swipe to turn a page

Swipe left to go to the next page and right to go back. If you've turned on Vertical Scrolling (⋯ › Page Display & Layout › Vertical Scrolling), the pages form one long column instead, and you scroll up and down through them.

To go further than one page in a single piece, tap the page to show the page thumbnails and tap the page you want, or tap Sections and choose a section.

Use a keyboard

With a keyboard connected to your iPad or iPhone, or on a Mac, these keys turn pages in any reader:

  • Next page: Right Arrow, Down Arrow, Page Down, Space or Command-Right Arrow.
  • Previous page: Left Arrow, Up Arrow, Page Up, Shift-Space, Return or Command-Left Arrow.
  • Escape closes the reader. In Performance Mode it leaves Performance Mode instead.

Keys don't turn pages while you're annotating, typing a note or customizing the toolbar, or while a sheet or popover is open over the reader. On iPad, hold down the Command key to see the reader's shortcuts.

Tip If the arrow keys turn the wrong way for you, turn on Swap Forward and Back in Account › Preferences › Page Turner. It changes the arrow keys as well as a pedal.

Read ahead with Half-Page Turns

Half-Page Turns split each page turn into two steps. The first press shows the top half of the next page above the bottom half of the page you're playing, so you can see what's coming while you finish the current page. The second press shows the whole next page. Turning back reverses one step at a time, so the other pedal or key always undoes the last press.

To turn it on, choose ⋯ › Page Display & Layout › Half-Page Turns. The setting applies to every piece, set list and Planning Center plan until you turn it off.

With Half-Page Turns on:

  • Keys, Bluetooth pedals and Face Gestures turn half a page at a time.
  • You can tap the edges of the page to turn: tap the right quarter to go forward and the left quarter to go back. A tap in the middle does what it did before, such as showing the page thumbnails.
  • A swipe still turns a whole page.
  • The line between the two halves goes in the white space between systems near the middle of the page, when there is some, so a system is less likely to be cut in two.

Half-Page Turns work only when you see one page at a time. While Vertical Scrolling is on, or while Two-Page View is showing two pages side by side, pages turn as usual and the menu item is dimmed with the reason. In a set list, intermission, announcement and other set-list pages always turn as whole pages.

Note When you tap Annotate during a half-page split, the reader goes back to a whole page first, so your markings land on the right page.
