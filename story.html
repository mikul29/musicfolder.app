Before the downbeat.

Is Music Folder free?

Yes. The free version holds up to 15 songs with the full reader and organizer, and iCloud sync is free for everyone. Premium adds unlimited songs and books plus the complete toolkit, from $2.99/month with a 7-day free trial.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Your music lives on your device, so you can read, play through set lists and use every tool with no signal at all. iCloud catches up the next time you're online.

Can my band share charts?

Yes. Share a folder or set list with other Music Folder users and it stays in sync: when someone adds a chart or reorders the set, everyone's copy updates. You choose view-only or can-edit for each person, and annotations stay personal.

Does it work with Planning Center?

Yes. Link your Planning Center Services account to see your team's song library as a folder and upcoming service plans beside your set lists, then play through a service song by song. Access is read-only: plans are edited in Planning Center itself.

Does it support Bluetooth page turners?

Yes. Pair a Bluetooth page-turner pedal and turn pages hands-free. You can also tap or swipe, read page by page or scroll continuously.

Can I use Apple Pencil?

Yes: Apple Pencil, any stylus or your finger. Add text stamps for fingerings, dynamics and cuts. Annotations save automatically and sync to your other devices.

What happens to my music if I cancel Premium?

Your PDFs stay in your library. The free-tier limits apply only to adding more songs.

Is there an Android, Mac or web version?

Music Folder is made for iPhone and iPad only.

How do I get help?

Email musicfolder.app@gmail.com. Music Folder is made by an independent developer, so you'll hear back from the person who builds it.
