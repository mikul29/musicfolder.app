Questions about organizing sheet music

How much music can I keep for free?

The free version holds 15 pieces, 5 sections and 1 folder. Premium removes every limit, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.

Can I keep a whole method book in one file?

Yes. Import the book as one PDF and add a section where each piece starts. You can open the book at any section and add single sections to a set list.

Can Music Folder find the pieces in a book for me?

If the PDF has its own table of contents, Music Folder suggests sections from it for you to review. On iPhone and iPad models with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), Find Pieces Automatically can also read the book's contents page and page titles and suggest sections. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.

Can I scan paper sheet music?

Yes. Scan pages with your iPhone or iPad camera and they're saved as a PDF in your library, ready to read, mark up and organize.

Does cropping change my PDF?

No. Crop margins only changes how pages are shown, so Reset always brings back the whole page. Organize Pages, on the other hand, really reorders, rotates, adds or removes pages.

Does my library sync between devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud account, across iPhone, iPad and Macs with Apple silicon. Syncing is free.
