Chord charts you can make your own.

Write a chart, import a ChordPro file or turn a PDF chord sheet into a chart you can edit. Then play it in the singer's key, with a capo, or as Nashville numbers.

From a lyric sheet to the stand.

  1. Write or import

    Type the chords in the lyrics, paste a chart, import a ChordPro file or convert a PDF chord sheet.

  2. Make it yours

    Put the sections in the order you play them, choose one column or two, and set the page size.

  3. Play it in any key

    Pick the singer's key or a capo, and the whole chart follows. Your original stays as written.

Write it, or bring it in.

The editor shows the chart beside a live preview of the printed page. Type chords inline or paste chords written above the lyrics, and Music Folder lines them up for you.

  • ChordPro files (.cho, .chordpro, .pro, .crd, .txt)
  • Title, artist, key, tempo and time at the top
  • One editable chart instead of a PDF per key

Turn a PDF chord sheet into a chart.

Convert a PDF chord sheet into an editable chart. Music Folder reads the chords and lyrics, sets out the sections, and marks any line it isn't sure about so you can check it against the original.

  • Compare the chart with the original PDF
  • Flagged lines to review before you save
  • Works on chord sheets, not staff notation

Any key, any capo.

Change the key for the singer or play with a capo, and every chord on the chart follows. Switch to Nashville numbers, or hide the chords for a lyric sheet.

  • Changing key or capo is part of Premium
  • Chords, Numbers or Lyrics
  • Your original key is kept as written

Sections in the order you play them.

Label verses, choruses and bridges, then set the order once. The order strip sits at the top of the chart, and Follow Sequence spells the song out in the order you play it.

  • One or two columns, fonts and text size
  • Page sizes from Letter and A4 to 16:9 screens
  • Sections numbered the way Planning Center numbers them

Straight into the set list.

Charts live in the same library as your PDFs, so a set can mix chord charts, lead sheets and full scores, each with its own key and tempo.

  • Charts and PDFs in one set
  • Share charts with your band (Premium)
  • Works with Planning Center charts too

Questions about chord charts

Is the chord chart editor free?

Yes. Writing, importing, converting and reading charts is free, and so is switching between Chords, Numbers and Lyrics. Changing a chart's key or capo is part of Premium.

Can I import ChordPro files?

Yes. Music Folder opens ChordPro files ending in .cho, .chordpro, .pro or .crd, and plain .txt chord sheets.

Which PDFs can it convert?

PDF chord sheets: chords written over lyrics, with readable text or a clear scan. After converting, you check any flagged lines against the original. Sheet music written on staves isn't converted.

Does changing the key change my chart?

No. The chart keeps the key it was written in, and the key or capo you choose is how you see it. You can go back to the original at any time.

Can I show Nashville numbers?

Yes. Choose Numbers to show every chord as a number in the key, or Lyrics to hide the chords.

Does it work with Planning Center?

Yes. With Planning Center connected (Premium), your team's charts open in the plan's key, and you can save one to your own library. See Planning Center.
