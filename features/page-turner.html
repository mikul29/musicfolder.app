Turn pages hands‑free.

Pair a Bluetooth page-turner pedal and keep both hands on your instrument. Pages turn in every score, and straight through your whole set list.

Set up in a minute.

  1. Pair your pedal

    Connect it in your iPhone or iPad’s Bluetooth settings, like any keyboard.

  2. Choose arrow keys

    Set the pedal to arrow keys or Page Up and Page Down. Most pedals have a mode for this.

  3. Open a score and play

    Press right for the next page and left for the previous one. Nothing to set up in the app.

Turn pages with your face.

No pedal? Hold a head turn, a wink or a mouth movement for a moment and the page turns.

Turn your head right for the next page, left to go back.

Relax — Face Gestures are ready.

  1. Set it up once

    A short guided setup learns your movement, then lets you practice turning a few pages. Setup and practice are free.

  2. Switch it on

    Turn on Face Gestures from ⋯ in any piece, set list or Planning Center plan.

  3. Play

    Hold right for the next page, left to go back. Relax between turns: each movement turns one page.

Face Gestures needs an iPhone or iPad with Face ID. Using it with your own music is part of Premium. Face tracking happens on your device; nothing is recorded or sent.

Straight through the set.

In a set list, a page turn on the last page carries straight into the next song, so the whole set plays like one score.

  • Works in every reader, Planning Center services included
  • Turn page by page, or scroll continuously
  • The screen stays awake while you play

Half-page turns, Page Links and more in the reader

Works with almost any pedal.

A Bluetooth page turner talks to your iPad like a small keyboard: each press sends a key, such as an arrow, Page Down or Space. Music Folder understands every key pedals use, so most turn pages as soon as they’re paired, with no setup. You can test yours, and swap its sides, in Account › Preferences › Page Turner.

  • Bluetooth keyboards work too
  • Turns pause while you annotate or type a note
  • Landscape for charts printed sideways

Questions about page turners

Which page-turner pedals work with Music Folder?

Bluetooth pedals that send arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Space or Return. That’s how most page turners work, including keyboard modes on popular pedals. Bluetooth keyboards work too.

Do I need to set anything up in the app?

No. Pair the pedal in your device’s Bluetooth settings and open a score. If pages don’t turn, switch the pedal to its arrow-key or Page Up and Page Down mode.

Does it work in set lists?

Yes. In a set list, turning past the last page of a song goes straight to the first page of the next one.

Can I turn half a page?

Yes. Turn on Half-Page Turns in ⋯ › Page Display & Layout. The first press shows the top of the next page above the bottom of the one you’re playing, and the second press finishes the turn. Back works the same way. It works when pages turn one at a time, not with Vertical Scrolling or Two-Page View.

Why does the on-screen keyboard disappear?

iPhone and iPad hide the on-screen keyboard while a keyboard-type pedal is connected. Turn the pedal off to type, then turn it back on.

Is page turning free?

Yes. Turning pages with a pedal works in the free version. Free for up to 15 pieces, 5 sections and 1 folder, with chord charts, backups and iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit, chord transposition, collaboration and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.

Can I turn pages without a pedal?

Yes. On an iPhone or iPad with Face ID, Face Gestures turns the page when you hold a head turn, a wink or a mouth movement for a moment. Setup and practice are free; using it with your own music is part of Premium.

Does Face Gestures record me?

No. Face tracking happens on your device, and only while Face Gestures is on or you’re setting it up. Nothing is recorded or sent.
