Questions about the toolkit

Which tools are in the toolkit?

A metronome, a chromatic tuner with a guitar mode, reference tones and drones, and an 88-key piano. Every song can also have a recording, and a daily practice streak counts your minutes.

Are the tools free?

The metronome, tuner, tones and piano are part of Premium, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan. Recordings and practice streaks are free for everyone.

Does the metronome keep playing when the screen locks?

Yes. The metronome and tones keep going when you lock your iPhone or switch apps, and they play even with the ringer on silent.

Can I change the tuner’s reference pitch?

Yes. Set A4 anywhere from 415 to 466 Hz, for baroque pitch or an ensemble that tunes high.

Which guitar tunings are included?

Standard, Drop D, Half Step Down, Whole Step Down, Drop C, DADGAD, Open G and Open D.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
