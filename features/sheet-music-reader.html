A sheet music reader built for the moment you perform.

Open any PDF and play from it with confidence: nothing gets bumped on stage, repeats and codas are one tap away, and the next line is in view before you turn.

Ready for the stage in a minute.

  1. Open a score

    Any PDF, scan or chord chart in your library, or a single piece from inside a book.

  2. Set it up once

    Add links for the repeats, choose half-page turns, crop the margins. It's remembered for next time.

  3. Perform

    Tap Perform Set or turn on Performance Mode, and play. Turn with a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth pedal.

Nothing gets bumped on stage.

Performance Mode hides the toolbars and turns the whole page into one big page-turn area, so a stray tap never opens a menu, zooms or starts a marking mid-song.

  • Tap the right of the page to go on, the left to go back
  • Start a whole set with Perform Set
  • Optional protection against double turns

The D.S. that doesn't send you hunting.

Link a D.S., D.C. or To Coda to where it goes. One tap jumps there, and Back returns you to where you left off.

  • Set up once per piece in a short guided flow
  • Links stay with the piece when you edit its pages
  • A broken link does nothing, never jumps somewhere wrong

Read across the page break.

With Half-Page Turns, the first press shows the top of the next page above the bottom of the one you're playing, so you can keep reading through the turn. The second press finishes it.

  • Works with a tap, a swipe or a pedal
  • Back works the same way, one step at a time
  • For pianists, accompanists and long parts

Two pages, side by side.

Turn your iPad sideways or widen the window on a Mac and see two pages at once, the way the music sits on a piano stand.

  • Two-Page View on iPad and Mac
  • Landscape and portrait on every device
  • Or scroll through the score continuously

Bigger notes, darker stage.

Crop away the white margins so the notation fills the screen, and switch on Dark Background for dim stages and late rehearsals: light notation on a dark page.

  • Auto-crop, then fine-tune with the handles
  • Your PDF is never changed; Reset brings the page back
  • Dark Background needs iOS 16 or later

Jump straight to the movement.

In a book or a multi-movement work, the Sections button lists every piece inside it. Tap one and the score opens at its first page, and the metronome follows that section's tempo.

  • Each section keeps its own key and tempo
  • Add sections while you read
  • Metronome playback is part of Premium

Your reader, your way.

Every reading choice lives in one menu. Pick vertical scrolling, two pages, half-page turns or a dark page, and arrange the toolbar so the tools you use sit within reach.

  • Customize the toolbar by dragging
  • Song notes and cues beside the score
  • Turn pages with a pedal or your face

Questions about the reader

Is the reader free?

Yes. Performance Mode, Page Links, Half-Page Turns, Two-Page View, Dark Background and cropping are all part of the free version. Metronome playback and Face Gestures in the reader are part of Premium.

What happens if I touch the page in Performance Mode?

Only page turns. A tap on the right of the page goes forward and a tap on the left goes back, so nothing else opens, zooms or draws. Hold Done to leave.

How do Page Links work?

In a piece, open ⋯ › Page Links and a short guided setup asks for the mark and the place it jumps to. While you play, tap the link to jump and tap Back to return.

Can I turn pages with a pedal?

Yes. Bluetooth page turners work in every reader, including Performance Mode and half-page turns. See the Page turner page for pedals and hands-free Face Gestures.

Can I read on my iPhone too?

Yes. The reader works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and on Macs with Apple silicon. Two-Page View needs the width of an iPad or Mac, and Dark Background needs iOS 16 or later.

Can I write on the score while I read?

Yes. Tap the pencil to mark up the page with Apple Pencil or your finger. See Annotation.
