A sheet music reader built for the moment you perform.

Open any PDF and perform with confidence. Keep your music front and center, navigate repeats with a tap, and turn pages without losing your place.

From score to stage, without the fuss.

  1. Open your music

    Open any PDF, scan or chord chart in your library, or go straight to one piece inside a book.

  2. Make it yours

    If you like, crop the margins, turn on half-page turns or link the repeats. It's all optional, and remembered for next time.

  3. Perform with confidence

    Turn on Performance Mode and play. Turn pages with a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth pedal.

Stay focused on the music.

Performance Mode clears the screen down to your score. The toolbars step aside and the whole page becomes a page-turn area, so a stray touch never opens a menu, zooms in or starts a marking in the middle of a piece.

  • Tap the right side to go on, the left to go back
  • Start a whole set list with Perform Set
  • Optional protection against accidental double turns

From D.S. to coda, in one tap.

Signs like D.S. (go back to the sign) and To Coda (skip ahead to the ending) usually mean hunting for the right page mid-performance. Link each sign to where it goes once, and from then on one tap takes you there.

  • Set up once per piece, in a short guided flow
  • Tap Back to return to exactly where you were
  • Links stay in place when you edit the pages

Read across the page break.

With Half-Page Turns, the first press brings in the top of the next page while the end of the current one stays in view, so you can keep reading through the turn. The second press finishes it.

  • Works with a tap, a swipe or a pedal
  • Back works the same way, one step at a time
  • For pianists, accompanists and long parts

Two pages, side by side.

Turn your iPad sideways or widen the window on a Mac and see two pages at once, the way the music sits on a piano stand.

  • Two-Page View on iPad and Mac
  • Landscape and portrait on every device
  • Or scroll through the score continuously

More music. Less margin.

Many PDFs carry wide white margins. Crop them away and the notation grows to fill the screen, easier to read from the stand. Cropping only changes how the page is shown; your PDF itself is never modified.

  • Auto finds the music; the handles fine-tune it
  • Crop one page or every page at once
  • Reset always brings back the whole page

One PDF, many pieces, instantly accessible.

Keep a hymnal, a method book or a multi-movement work as one PDF. The Sections button lists every piece inside; tap one and you're on its first page. Each section keeps its own key and tempo, and the metronome follows the tempo of the section you're in.

  • A checkmark shows the piece you're in
  • Add a section while you read
  • Metronome playback is part of Premium

Your reader, your way.

Read a page at a time, scroll continuously or see two pages side by side. Put the tools you reach for on the toolbar, in the order you like, and leave the rest in the menu, so the score keeps the space.

  • Add, remove and reorder toolbar tools
  • Single pages, vertical scrolling or Two-Page View
  • Portrait or landscape, on iPhone, iPad and Mac

A page that suits the room.

On a dim stage or late at night, switch on Dark Background for light notation on a dark page. It changes only how the page looks on screen: your PDF, thumbnails and exports stay as they are.

  • On or off from the reader's menu, in every reader
  • Works with Performance Mode, crops and your markings
  • Needs iOS 16 or later

Everything else within reach.

  • Notes beside the score

    Keep reminders for each piece in a notes panel next to the music, there whenever you open it.

  • Hands-free page turns

    Use a Bluetooth pedal, or Face Gestures with Premium. Page turner

  • Write on the page

  • Play along with a recording

    Attach a recording to a piece and play it from the reader, slowed down or in another key.

  • Tuner, metronome and piano

    The practice tools open over the score, without leaving it. Part of Premium. Toolkit

Questions about the reader

Is the reader free?

Yes. Performance Mode, Page Links, Half-Page Turns, Two-Page View, Dark Background and cropping are all part of the free version. Metronome playback and Face Gestures in the reader are part of Premium.

What happens if I touch the page in Performance Mode?

Only page turns. A tap on the right of the page goes forward and a tap on the left goes back, so nothing else opens, zooms or draws. Hold Done to leave.

How do Page Links work?

In a piece, open ⋯ › Page Links and a short guided setup asks for the sign and the place it jumps to. While you play, tap the link to jump and tap Back to return.

Can I turn pages with a pedal?

Yes. Bluetooth page turners work in every reader, including Performance Mode and half-page turns. See the Page turner page for pedals and hands-free Face Gestures.

Can I read on my iPhone too?

Yes. The reader works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and on Macs with Apple silicon. Two-Page View needs the width of an iPad or Mac, and Dark Background needs iOS 16 or later.

Can I write on the score while I read?

Yes. Tap the pencil to mark up the page with Apple Pencil or your finger. See Annotation.
