The set list app that holds your music.

Build the whole performance from your own music: full scores or single movements, intermissions and announcements, timed to the minute. Then play it straight through, one page after another.

Build a set in a minute.

  1. Pick the music

    Choose pieces from your library, a whole book or just the movements you’re playing. Playing one twice? Add it twice.

  2. Shape the show

    Drag to reorder, add an intermission or an announcement, and give the set a date. It waits on your Home screen when it’s next.

  3. Play it through

    The whole set opens as one score, with what’s up next. Turn pages with a swipe or a Bluetooth pedal.

For every kind of set.

A gig on Friday, a service on Sunday, a concert next month. Every set list keeps its own music, order and date.

  • A date for every set list
  • The next one on Home, marked Today or Tomorrow
  • Key and tempo on every row

Whole pieces, or just one movement.

Add a whole score, or open its sections and pick only the songs or movements you’re playing, straight from a hymnal, fake book or sonata. No splitting PDFs by hand.

  • Each section plays with its own tempo and time signature
  • Already-added sections are ticked
  • Open the whole document from any section

How sections work

Plan the whole show, to the minute.

Give each piece a length and the set adds up the total. Put pages between pieces for the intermission, an announcement or a note to the band, and give a break a length to see it count down on stage.

  • Lengths for every piece and the whole set
  • Intermission, Announcement, Note and Blank Page
  • White or black pages, so a break stays dark on stage

Perform it straight through.

Tap Perform Set and the whole set opens as one score in Performance Mode: the music fills the screen, every tap turns a page, and the next piece follows the last page of the one before.

  • Turn with a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth pedal
  • Breaks and announcement pages play in their place
  • Page Links and half-page turns work in the set too

Questions about set lists

Can the same song be in a set list twice?

Yes. Add it as many times as you play it.

Can I add one movement or song from a book?

Yes. Keep the book as one PDF and mark a section where each piece starts. In a set list, open the book’s sections and add only the ones you need. Playback shows just those pages, at each section’s own tempo.

Can I add an intermission or an announcement?

Yes. On the Pages tab, add an Intermission, Announcement, Note or Blank Page, in white or black. Give it a length and a countdown shows on stage.

Does it add up how long the set is?

Yes. Give each piece a length, or let a recording fill it in, and the set list shows the total time.

Can I share a set list with my band?

Yes. Share it live with people who use Music Folder and choose who can edit (Premium for everyone taking part), or send it as one PDF with your markings included.

Can I add Planning Center songs to a set list?

Yes. With Planning Center connected, you can mix your team’s songs with your own charts in one set. Planning Center is part of Premium.

How much does it cost?

Set lists are included in the free version. Free for up to 15 pieces, 5 sections and 1 folder, with chord charts, backups and iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit, chord transposition, collaboration and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
