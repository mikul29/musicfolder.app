Questions about annotation

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. You can write with your finger on iPhone and iPad. Apple Pencil gives you finer control on iPad.

Can I type on the score?

Yes. Add text boxes for cues and reminders, in several fonts.

Do my markings sync to my other devices?

Yes, for your own music and Planning Center charts. On music someone has shared with you, your markings stay on the device you wrote them on.

Can I share a marked-up copy?

Yes. Share a song or set list as a PDF with your markings included.

Is annotation free?

Yes. Annotation is part of the free version. Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
