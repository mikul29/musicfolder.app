Questions about free sheet music

Is the sheet music really free?

Yes. Browsing and adding scores is free. Each score you add counts as a song in your library, so the free version holds up to 15; Premium removes the limit.

What kind of music is in the catalog?

Classical and public-domain music: songs for voice and piano, classical guitar, string quartets, and pieces for piano, woodwinds and more. Pop, worship and film music are under copyright, so they aren’t included. You can import your own PDFs of those.

Where does the music come from?

From OpenScore, the Mutopia Project and the Internet Archive. Each piece is in the public domain or under an open license that allows you to use and share it.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to browse and add scores. Once a score is in your library, it works offline like the rest of your music.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
