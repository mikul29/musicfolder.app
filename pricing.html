Questions about pricing

Is Music Folder really free?

Yes. The free version is free forever: up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, set lists, the reader, annotation, sharing and iCloud sync.

Is there a free trial of Premium?

Yes. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You won’t be charged until it ends, and you can cancel before then.

How do I cancel?

Premium is billed through your Apple Account. Cancel anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, and Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my music if I stop Premium?

Nothing is deleted. Your songs, books, folders and set lists stay in your library; the free limit only applies to adding more.

Does everyone in my group need Premium?

No. Sharing is free, and music shared with you doesn’t count toward the free version’s 15-song limit. Each person only needs Premium for the toolkit or Planning Center.

I bought Music Folder before version 3.0. Do I need to pay again?

No. If you bought Music Folder before version 3.0, you keep full access.

Are prices the same everywhere?

Prices are shown in US dollars and may vary by region. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.
