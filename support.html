Getting started

What is Music Folder?

A sheet music app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Store your PDFs, sort them into folders, build set lists, mark up scores with Apple Pencil, and practice with a built-in metronome, tuner and piano. Everything syncs through iCloud.

Which devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later), where Music Folder runs as the iPad app. iPad is recommended for reading because of its larger screen, and it has its own sidebar layout on iPadOS 18 and later. There is no Android or web version. See Use Music Folder on a Mac.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 pieces, 5 sections and 1 folder. Premium unlocks unlimited music, sections and folders, the complete toolkit, chord transposition, collaboration and Planning Center: $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $49.99 for lifetime access. If you bought Music Folder before version 3.0, you keep full access. iCloud sync is free for everyone. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region. More in Free and Premium.

What happens to my music if I cancel Premium?

Your music stays in your library. The free limits (15 pieces, 5 sections and 1 folder) only apply to adding more. To cancel, see Manage your subscription.

How do I get Premium back on a new device?

It usually comes back on its own when you open the app, as long as the device uses the Apple Account you bought it with. If it doesn't, open Account, tap Upgrade to Premium, then tap Restore Purchases. See Manage your subscription.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Your music lives on your device, so you can read, play through set lists and use every tool with no signal. iCloud catches up the next time you're online.

Music, sections and set lists

What are sections?

A section marks where a piece starts and ends inside a PDF. Use them for a PDF with many pieces in it, like a hymnal, fake book or method book, or for the movements of a longer work. Tap a section to jump straight to it, and add any section to a set list on its own. See Add sections to a book.

Where did bookmarks go?

Bookmarks are now called sections. Nothing else changed: your bookmarks are all still there as sections. To see them, open the PDF and tap the list button at the top left, next to the back arrow. See Add sections to a book.

Are sections the same as a verse or chorus?

No. The verses and choruses you type in a chord chart (V1, C1 and so on) are part of the chart itself. Sections in a PDF mark where each piece starts. They share a name but they're separate features. See Set the song order for chord-chart sections.

What's a set list?

An ordered list of music for a performance, service or rehearsal. In playback, everything runs together so you can page straight through the whole set with a swipe, a Bluetooth pedal or your keyboard. See Create and edit a set list.

Can I put one piece from a book in a set list?

Yes. When you add music to a set list, choose either the whole PDF or one of its sections. The set list plays only that section's pages. See Create and edit a set list.

Importing your music

Can I import several PDFs at once?

Yes. In your library, tap + and choose Files, then select as many PDFs as you like, or choose Folder to bring in a whole folder at once. Every way to bring music in is in Import music.

How do I import a book with many pieces in one PDF?

Import it as one PDF, then add a section where each piece starts. The steps, including Find Pieces Automatically, are in Add sections to a book.

Can I scan paper sheet music?

Yes. Tap + and choose Scan Sheet Music to scan pages with your camera. See Import music.

Using the app

How do I annotate with Apple Pencil?

Open a piece and tap the pencil button in the toolbar, then draw with Apple Pencil or your finger. Tap the pencil again when you're done. See Mark up music with Apple Pencil and Use annotation layers.

How do I use the metronome?

Open any piece and tap the metronome button. It uses the tempo saved with the piece. Playing the metronome is part of Premium. See Use the metronome.

Does it work with a Bluetooth page turner?

Yes. Pedals connect as a Bluetooth keyboard, so set yours to a mode that sends the arrow keys or Page Up and Page Down; media and volume modes aren't supported. You can test it in Account under Preferences › Page Turner. See Turn pages hands-free, or My page turner isn't turning pages if it doesn't turn.

Can I transpose a chord chart?

Yes. Open the chart and tap Key in the toolbar to choose another key, a capo or Nashville numbers. Changing the key or capo requires Music Folder Premium; viewing charts in their saved key is free. See Transpose, capo and numbers.

Does it work with Planning Center?

Yes. Connect your Planning Center Services account to see your team's songs as a folder and upcoming services next to your set lists, then play through a service song by song. Planning Center is part of Premium, and access is read-only: plans are still edited in Planning Center. See Connect Planning Center.

Sync, backup and sharing

How do I share music with my band or class?

Two ways. Share a folder or set list live with other Music Folder users: changes show up for everyone, and you choose who can edit. Or send a one-time copy of a song, folder or set list as a file through AirDrop, Messages or email; the recipient taps it to import. Sharing live is part of Premium for everyone taking part; a one-time copy is free. See Share a folder or set list and Send a copy or print.

How do I sync between my iPad and iPhone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices. In Settings → [your name] → iCloud, make sure Music Folder is turned on in the list of apps using iCloud. Sync then happens automatically in the background. See Sync with iCloud.

My music isn't syncing. What should I check?

In order: the same iCloud account on both devices, Music Folder turned on under iCloud, enough iCloud storage, and a working internet connection. The first sync of a large library can take several minutes. The full checklist is in My music isn't syncing.

How do I back up my library?

Open Account, then Library & Sync › Backup & Restore, and tap Back Up Now. You can also turn on automatic backups there. A backup holds your music, folders, set lists, notes and markings, and, if you choose, your recordings. See Back up and restore your library.

My library is empty after reinstalling. What should I do?

Give iCloud time to download it, and check that you're signed in with the same Apple Account as before. If it doesn't come back, restore a backup saved in iCloud Drive (backups saved On This Device are deleted with the app). See My library looks empty after reinstalling.

Privacy

What data does Music Folder collect?

None. Your music and notes stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. Read the full privacy policy.
