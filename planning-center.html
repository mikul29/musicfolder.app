Already using Music Stand, Planning Center's own reader? Music Folder is for musicians who also keep a library of their own: your PDFs, fake books, folders and set lists sit next to your team's services, with a metronome, tuner and piano built in.

Questions about Planning Center

What do I need to connect?

A Planning Center Services account with access to your church's songs or plans, and Music Folder Premium (try it free for 7 days).

Can Music Folder change our plans?

No. Access is read-only. Plans, songs and arrangements are still edited in Planning Center, and Music Folder shows the latest version.

Does it work offline?

Charts you've opened are kept on the device, so open the service once before you get to church. Your own music works completely offline.

What happens if I disconnect?

Your own songs, folders and set lists stay exactly as they are. Only the Planning Center songs and services are removed from view.

Is Music Folder made by Planning Center?

No. Music Folder is an independent app that connects to Planning Center Services with your permission.
