The sheet music app for worship teams.

No more printed charts, stapled set lists or last-minute texts. Keep every chord chart and lyric sheet in one library and share Sunday’s set with the team.

Ready before Sunday.

  1. Bring in your charts

    Import chord charts and lyric sheets from Files, Mail or AirDrop, or scan paper copies with your camera. Sort them into folders by team or season.

  2. Build Sunday’s set

    Add songs from your library in the order you’ll lead them, or open the service straight from Planning Center.

  3. Share it with the team

    Everyone gets the set on their own iPhone, iPad or Mac. Change the order and it updates for everyone right away.

Sunday’s set, shared.

Share a set list or a folder of charts with your musicians and singers. You choose who can edit, and every change reaches the whole team.

  • Live sharing with everyone who uses Music Folder

  • Mark up your own copy with Apple Pencil

Come prepared.

Attach a recording to every song, from your music library or a file, and play it right beside the chart. Musicians learn how it goes before rehearsal, and the drummer counts in at the song’s tempo.

  • Tuner, drones and piano one tap away

Questions from worship teams

Do I need Planning Center?

No. Import your chord charts and lyric sheets, sort them into folders and build Sunday's set list yourself. If your church uses Planning Center, you can connect it too.

Does Music Folder work with Planning Center?

Yes. Connect your Planning Center Services account and your team's songs appear as a folder, with upcoming services next to your set lists. Access is read-only: plans are still edited in Planning Center.

How can the team hear how a song goes?

Attach a recording to each song, from your music library or a file, and play it beside the chart. When you send a song to someone as a file, its recording goes with it. With Planning Center, audio attached to the plan plays right in the reader.

Can the whole team use it?

Yes. Share a set list or a folder of your charts with your musicians and singers who use Music Folder, and choose who can edit. Each musician can also connect their own Planning Center account.

Does it work if the church Wi-Fi drops?

Your own music works completely offline. Planning Center charts you've opened are saved on the device, so open the service once before Sunday.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
