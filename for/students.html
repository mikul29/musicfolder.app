Questions from students and soloists

Is there a free version?

Yes, for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with set lists, the reader, Apple Pencil and iCloud sync. Premium adds unlimited music and the full toolkit.

Which practice tools are included?

A metronome with tap tempo and subdivisions, a chromatic tuner, tones and drones for intonation, an 88-key sampled piano, recordings for every song and a daily practice streak. The tools are part of Premium.

Can a teacher or accompanist share music with me?

Yes. When they share a folder or set list with you, it appears in your library. Joining is free, and shared music doesn't count toward the free version's 15-song limit.

Where can I find music to practice?

Import your own PDFs, or use Discover to bring in free public-domain scores in one tap.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. iPad is best for reading full pages, and iPhone keeps the same library and tools in your pocket.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
