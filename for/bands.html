Questions from bands

Can the whole band use the same set list?

Yes. Share a set list or folder with the other members who use Music Folder, and choose for each person whether they can edit or only view. When the set changes, everyone's copy updates. You can also send a one-time copy as a file through AirDrop, Messages or email.

Can I turn pages with a foot pedal?

Yes. Bluetooth page-turner pedals and keyboards work in every reader, and in a set list the pedal carries straight on into the next song, so your hands stay on your instrument. You can also swipe.

Does it work without Wi-Fi at the venue?

Completely. Your music is stored on your device, so you can read and play through a set with no signal. iCloud catches up the next time you're online.

Can I use songs from a songbook in a set list?

Yes. Import the songbook as one PDF, add a bookmark for each song, then put the bookmarks in your set. Playback shows only the bookmarked pages.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
