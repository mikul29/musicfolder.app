Questions from ensembles

How do I share music with the whole ensemble?

Build the concert program as a set list, or keep the season's repertoire in a folder, and share it with your players or singers who use Music Folder. Choose for each person whether they can edit or only view, and changes appear on everyone's device.

Can I use it with a school band or class?

Yes. Directors share a folder with the class, with students on view only, so everyone has the same music at rehearsal and at home. Joining a shared folder is free, and shared music doesn't count toward the free version's 15-song limit.

Can I keep a whole score or choral collection in one file?

Yes. Import it as a book and add a bookmark for each piece or movement to jump straight to it.

Does it work on iPhone as well as iPad?

Yes. iPad is best for reading full pages; iPhone keeps the same library in your pocket, synced through iCloud.

How much does it cost?

Free for up to 15 songs, 1 book and 1 folder, with iCloud sync. Premium unlocks unlimited music, the complete toolkit and Planning Center: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $2.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by region.
