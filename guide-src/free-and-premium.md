Music Folder is free to use with a small library, and the free version has no time limit. Premium removes the limits on pieces, sections and folders, and adds the practice tools, chord chart transposition, collaboration and Planning Center.

## What's free

The free version covers reading and organizing a small library:

- Up to **15 pieces**, **5 sections** (counted across your whole library) and **1 folder**
- Set lists, including playing them straight through
- The reader, Performance Mode, Two-Page View and Bluetooth page turners
- Chord charts: writing, importing and reading them
- Annotation with Apple Pencil, annotation layers, and notes on each piece
- Recordings attached to your pieces
- Free sheet music from the built-in catalog
- Backups and iCloud sync
- Sending a one-time copy of a piece, folder or set list to someone else

The two example pieces in Account › Support › Examples don't count toward the limits, and Premium tools such as Transpose work in them for free.

## What Premium adds

- Unlimited music, sections and folders
- The metronome, the tuner, reference tones and drones, and the 88-key piano
- Transposing a chord chart and setting a capo (reading a chart in its written key is free)
- Face Gesture page turning on devices that support it (setting it up and trying it are free)
- Collaboration on folders and set lists
- Planning Center Services (you also need a Planning Center Services account)

Collaboration is Premium for everyone taking part: the person who shares a folder or set list and each person who joins it. Sending someone a one-time copy is free.

To see this comparison in the app, open Account, tap **Upgrade to Premium**, then tap **Compare Free and Premium**.

## Plans and the free trial

Premium is the same whichever way you pay:

- **Annual**: $19.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial
- **Monthly**: $2.99 a month
- **Lifetime**: $49.99, paid once

Prices are in US dollars and vary by region; the app and the App Store always show the price in your currency before you buy. The free trial is on the annual plan and is offered once per Apple Account. While you're eligible, the purchase button reads **Start 7-day free trial**, and the line under it gives the price that applies after the trial.

[Manage your subscription](/guide/manage-your-subscription) explains how to subscribe, switch plans and cancel.

## When you reach a limit

When something would go past a free limit, such as adding a 16th piece, a second folder or a sixth section, the app shows the Premium screen instead and nothing is changed. Tap {sf:xmark} to close it and carry on with the free version.

## If Premium ends

Nothing is deleted. Your music, sections, folders and set lists stay in your library even if there are more than the free limits allow; the limits only apply to adding more. The Premium tools lock again. Folders and set lists you share with others, or that others share with you, aren't left or changed, but they don't open until you have Premium again.

## If you bought Premium before Music Folder 3.0

If you bought the original one-time Premium upgrade before version 3.0, you keep Premium for good and don't need to buy it again. On a new device, open Account, tap **Upgrade to Premium**, then tap **Restore Purchases**. Account then shows your plan as **Lifetime**.
