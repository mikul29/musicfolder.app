Premium is sold through the App Store and billed to your Apple Account. You subscribe in Music Folder, and Apple handles billing, renewal and cancellation; the app's Account screen takes you to each of them.

## Subscribe

1. Open Account: tap your picture or initials at the top of Home, or, where the app shows a sidebar, your name or **Account** at the bottom of it.
2. Tap **Upgrade to Premium**.
3. Choose **Annual**, **Monthly** or **Lifetime**. Each shows its price in your currency.
4. Tap the button at the bottom. It reads **Start 7-day free trial** when the trial is available to you, otherwise **Get Annual Premium**, **Get Monthly Premium** or **Get Lifetime Premium**.
5. Confirm the purchase with Apple.

The same Premium screen opens when you reach a free limit or tap a Premium tool. The line under the purchase button always states what you'll be charged and whether the plan renews. Lifetime is a single payment with no renewals.

## Check your plan

When you have Premium, Account shows a **Premium** row with your plan's name. Tap it to see your plan and its status, for example the next renewal date, the date a canceled subscription ends, or when a free trial's first payment is due. If Apple can't take a payment, it says so here too.

## Restore a purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, Premium usually comes back on its own when you open the app. If it doesn't:

1. Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought Premium with.
2. Open Account and tap **Upgrade to Premium**.
3. Tap **Restore Purchases** at the bottom of the screen.

If you already have Premium and want to refresh it, tap **Premium** in Account, then **Restore Purchases**. The app tells you **Purchases Restored** when it finds your purchase, or **Nothing to Restore** if this Apple Account has none.

## Change plans

1. In Account, tap **Premium**.
2. Under **Change Plan**, tap **Monthly**, **Yearly** or **Lifetime**. (Yearly is the plan called Annual on the purchase screen. Change Plan appears while you have a subscription.)

A new subscription plan starts when your current one renews, and you keep Premium in between. Lifetime takes effect as soon as you buy it. After buying Lifetime, cancel your subscription so it doesn't renew; the Premium screen reminds you while one is still active.

## Cancel your subscription

1. In Account, tap **Premium**.
2. Tap **Manage Subscription**.
3. In Apple's subscription sheet that opens, cancel the subscription.

You can also cancel without opening the app: in Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for, and if you're in a free trial, canceling before it ends means you aren't charged. Apple's article [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) covers every device.

When Premium ends, nothing in your library is deleted. See [Free and Premium](/guide/free-and-premium) for what changes.

On Mac: **Manage Subscription** opens your App Store subscriptions page.

## Redeem an offer code

1. In Account, tap **Redeem Offer Code**.
2. Enter the code in Apple's sheet.

Premium turns on once Apple confirms the code. On a Mac, Redeem Offer Code opens the App Store to enter it.
